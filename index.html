Cohort readiness check

Check who has finished a given topic before a cohort starts (defaults to Start Here: The Essential Toolkit for the incoming cohort) and see who's not started or in progress. Switch to Individual challenge to check one specific challenge instead — useful for topics like Texas/Florida Contract Law, where knowing exactly which challenge someone still needs points to the specific training gap rather than just an overall percentage. Click "Show training & resume" on any VA to see their full topic progress and resume on file, without leaving this tab — handy for seeing what skills a completed cohort collectively has.
This cohort isn't tied to a fixed start date, so there's no single "before the cohort starts" cutoff — this view just ranks everyone by how far along they are on Start Here: The Essential Toolkit, furthest along first. Bilingual Generalist Certification was retired Oct 14, 2026 as a bilingual-status check (it wasn't reliably identifying Spanish speakers — some who completed it don't speak Spanish), so this view is Essential Toolkit progress only for now, pending a proper Languages Spoken field. Useful both for spotting who's placement-ready and for seeing who's moving fastest, e.g. if you're sizing up a potential Spanish trainer from within the cohort.

Attrition-risk watch: placed VAs

For the bilingual LATAM bench specifically: we hire unexperienced VAs and train them up, so some turnover is expected once a placed VA clears the experience bar that competitors like Job Duck or Virtual Latinos require. This looks at each currently-placed VA's actual topic activity dates (not just the change since last refresh), so a slow burn spread across several weekly updates still shows up. One topic touched recently is normal — they may have found a gap, or you or the agent asked them to take it. Multiple different topics touched in the same window is the real signal: it looks like deliberate certificate-collecting for a resume, so those are flagged at the top. It's a proxy, not proof — a prompt to check in, not a verdict.

Gametize accounts with no roster match

People show up in Gametize before they show up on the roster — VAs typically join with a personal email before their training cohort starts, and the official VRA email is only issued once the cohort begins (so a roster entry may simply not exist yet). This view surfaces every Gametize account that doesn't match a roster VA by email or name, with when they joined Gametize and their training progress since, so you can tell a brand-new pre-cohort signup from an old account that's gone quiet. Recently joined (14 days or less) is the expected, healthy case for someone about to start or just starting. Settling in (15–45 days) is worth a glance if progress looks stalled. Long-standing, no roster match (45+ days) is the one to review — either they were never added to the roster, or they may no longer be with the company. This is a proxy based on join date and activity, not a verdict — confirm with the actual roster/HR record before acting on it.
